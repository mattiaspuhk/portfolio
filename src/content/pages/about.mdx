I'm Mattias. I'm from Estonia and live in London, where I'm doing an MSc in Management of Information Systems and Digital Innovation at LSE.

Before that I spent almost two years as a full-stack engineer at RingIT, a tech consultancy in Tallinn. I worked on GAIA, a partner relationship management platform, and was the top contributor to its rebuild. I built an import that turns spreadsheets and business-card photos into contacts, and an analyst that answers questions about an organisation's own data. In both, the model proposes and the user decides.

I studied Business Information Technology at TalTech in Tallinn, with an exchange semester at the Technical University of Munich. My thesis compared retrieval-augmented generation approaches for a B2B SaaS platform, using a custom evaluation harness. The findings informed the platform's AI rebuild.

Outside work I photograph commercial aircraft. I speak Estonian and English, and conversational Italian, French and Russian.
